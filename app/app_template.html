Resistivity probe calibration surfaces

Two independent laboratory calibrations of in-situ resistivity probes, and what one scale factor does and does not reconcile between them. Conductivity is the reciprocal of the probe's voltage channel; NaCl is in mmol/kg.

Method

Each surface is fitted the way its own data warrants rather than both being forced through one algorithm. The 2000 experiment sampled four temperatures per isohaline across a conductivity curve that plateaus near 300 °C and falls away sharply above 350 °C; interpolating those raw points directly underestimates conductivity through the plateau by 12–28%, so the original spline pre-pass along temperature is reused. Both surfaces return no value outside the convex hull of their own measurements, so every node compared above stands on real data.
